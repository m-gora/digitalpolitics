# Internationalization
Internationalization is not needed as the sofware is purpose built for the german political system. Therefore the user facing language shall be german.

## Accessibility
The platform is primarily an internal tool for party staff and campaigners; accessibility (WCAG/BFSG) compliance is not required for the modules specified so far. This shall be revisited once the (future) public-facing street campaign module is specced, since the Barrierefreiheitsstärkungsgesetz may apply to that public-facing surface.

The crowdsourcing respondent app (crowd-02) is the first public-facing surface; its pragmatic accessibility baseline is defined there. Whether BFSG applies remains an open legal question.
