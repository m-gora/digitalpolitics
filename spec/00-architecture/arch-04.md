# Privacy By Design
Tracking Data and PII are strictly separated.
PII in street feedback shall be scrubbed automatically.

The concrete definition of PII in this context and the scrubbing mechanism are deferred to the street feedback module's own spec, once that module is detailed. This document establishes the principle; that principle is binding on that module's design regardless of implementation choice.

The PII definition and scrubbing mechanism for the crowdsourcing module are specified in crowd-03.
